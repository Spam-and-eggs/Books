    The Story

    Meet Agent Spam — mysterious, mustached, and fostered in the Scottish Highlands by two well-meaning bottles of ketchup. Meet Private Eggs — a deeply confident egg who insists he's "free range" and has never once, to anyone's knowledge, left Yorkshire. Add a cockney slice of toast with anger issues, an American bottle of brown sauce with a grudge against his own cousin, and one quietly furious pat of butter who has had enough of being forgotten.

    Spam and Eggs: A Very Serious Morning is a darkly funny, deadpan illustrated tale about routine, denial, and the food chain — told with the straight-faced absurdity of a bloke down the pub who swears every word of it is true.

    It probably isn't.

    Meet the Cast

    Agent Spam
    Status: Incognito

    Fostered in the Scottish Highlands by two well-meaning bottles of ketchup. Officially, nobody knows what he did before the tin.

    "A less salty mission, today."

    Private Eggs
    Status: Active, technically

    Deeply confident. Insists he's free range. Has never once, to anyone's knowledge, left Yorkshire.

    "Eggs does not stress, mon ami."

    Dr. Brown Sauce
    Status: Dead centre, always

    Thick and heavy — not at all like his cousin A1, a distant, should-be-forgotten diner regular.

    "That's my prerogative."
